About Inkwell Shelf

Inkwell Shelf is a small library of thirty complete short stories. Each one fits in a single chapter, so you can finish a story in one sitting and move on to the next.

How to use the shelf

  • Search by title or author, or filter by genre on the home page.
  • Use Details to preview a story, then Read to open it.
  • Like the stories you enjoy. Your likes are saved on this device.
  • In a story, show the synopsis, adjust the text size, and move to the previous or next story.
  • Switch between light and dark mode at any time.

Images

Covers are in images/covers and scene illustrations are in images/scenes. They are placeholders and can be replaced with your own artwork using the same file names.
